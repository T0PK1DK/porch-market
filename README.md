# Porch Market

Free storefronts for home vendors and cottage food makers.

Browse nearby porches, open your own, and ask a kitchen to hold something for pickup. Listings and holds stay in the browser that created them — there are no accounts yet.

## What’s in the app

- Browse with search and category filters
- Sample South Florida kitchens (sourdough, honey, cookies, eggs, hot sauce)
- A storefront with pickup notes and hold requests
- “Open a porch” with a live preview, saved on this device

Check cottage food rules before you sell. Porch Market is a listing, not a license or a payment processor.
