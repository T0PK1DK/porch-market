# Porch Market

Free storefronts for home vendors and cottage food makers. Browse nearby porches, save one, ask a kitchen to hold something for pickup, and pay the maker directly. Listings and holds stay in the browser that created them — there are no accounts.

This is a listing, not a license or a payment processor.

## What’s in the app

- Browse with search, category filters, **Open today**, and **Saved**
- A stylized South Florida **block map** for neighborhood filters, plus a **street map** (MapLibre GL + OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap tiles, no API key)
- Opt-in pins: home porches stay **approximate**; shops and food trucks may drop an exact pin. Truck “here today” pins expire at the end of the day
- Shop **order-ahead** on top of holds (`ready for pickup`, pay in the shop)
- Weekly stock counts and “spoken for” when a bake is claimed
- Pickup windows parsed from the kitchen’s own note — open now / next window
- Community kitchen marks: free, pay what you can, or donation-backed, plus an optional plates-this-week count
- Dietary notes on items (vegetarian, vegan, nut-free kitchen, and similar)
- Maker payment handles (Cash App, Venmo, PayPal.me, Zelle) — paid at pickup by default
- A **hold board**: kitchen tickets and the holds you placed
- A local porch feed on this device, with relative times
- Sample South Florida kitchens, including a community table
- “Open a porch” with a live preview, saved on this device
